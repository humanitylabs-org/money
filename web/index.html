Connect your bank first

Finances reads your accounts from a bank connection app. Install SimpleFIN from the App Store, follow its four steps, and your transactions show up here.

No bank connection? Start right away: import a file you downloaded from your bank's website (CSV, OFX or QFX), or add spending by hand.
